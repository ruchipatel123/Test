Precast

Box Culverts

RCPA manufactures precast reinforced concrete box culverts for civil drainage and stormwater applications. Designed for durability and rapid installation on site, our box culverts meet Australian Standards and Roads WA requirements.

Product Specifications

  • Applications: Stormwater drainage, road crossings, channel linings and infrastructure projects.
  • Compliance: Manufactured to AS/NZS standards and Roads WA specifications.
  • Custom sizing: Available in a range of spans and heights to suit project requirements.
  • Delivery: Supplied across Perth metro and regional Western Australia.
