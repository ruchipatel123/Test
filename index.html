Who we are

Engineering the pipes and precast that WA is built on

RCPA is Australia's only nationally operated, Australian-owned pipe company, specialising in the supply of vertically cast steel-reinforced concrete pipes. Formed in 2009 through the merger of Reinforced Concrete Pipes (RCP) and Australasian Pipeline & Precast (APP), RCPA is committed to excellence in manufacturing, technology, and innovation. The company pioneered the vertical cast pipe process in Australia and remains the sole national supplier of this product.
Driven by core values of safety, community, and environmental protection, RCPA adheres to strict compliance with regulations. With a focus on quality, efficiency, and sustainability, they continuously improve their products through advanced technologies.
RCPA also supports the industry through active involvement in associations like the Concrete Pipe Association of Australasia (CPAA) and the National Precast Concrete Association of Australia (NPCAA). Their values, which emphasise being at the forefront of innovation, guide everything they do.
