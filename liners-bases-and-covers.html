Liners, Bases & Covers

RCPA supplies a comprehensive range of precast concrete liners, pit bases and covers for stormwater, drainage and utility applications. Manufactured for strength, durability and compliance with Australian Standards.

Product Specifications

  • Liners: Precast concrete liners for pits, channels and drainage structures.
  • Bases: Reinforced pit bases designed for load-bearing applications.
  • Covers: Heavy-duty covers for access pits and infrastructure openings.
  • Compliance: Manufactured to AS/NZS standards with quality assurance testing.
